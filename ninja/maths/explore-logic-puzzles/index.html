Detective and Machine

Two kinds of puzzle. In one you find a number using clues. In the other you crack a secret rule. Endless puzzles, no timer.

I'm thinking of a number…

    How to play. Tap a number to cross it out (tap again to bring it back). Drag your finger to cross out lots at once. Cross out every number that breaks a clue until only one is left. Detective tip: start with the clue that rules out the most numbers. And look at the grid's patterns: multiples of 10 make a column, multiples of 9 make a diagonal.

    Feed the machine

    Name the rule

    Scientist tip. Don't put in random numbers. Try 1, then 2, then 3. How much does the answer jump each time? That jump tells you what the machine multiplies by. Then work out what else it adds or takes away.
