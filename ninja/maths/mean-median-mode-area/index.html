Exam favourite: the missing number

If the mean of 5 numbers is 6, the total must be 6 × 5 = 30. Add up the numbers you know, then take that away from 30 to find the one that is missing.

Check these every time

  • Is the height straight up, square to the base?
  • Radius or diameter? Radius is half the diameter.
  • Are the units squared? Area is in cm², m², and so on.
