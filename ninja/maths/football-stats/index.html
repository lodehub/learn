What each one tells you

Mean — total goals divided by number of players. Good for "how many goals does the team score, on average". But one superstar scoring loads of goals can make the mean look better than what most players actually did.

Median — line up every player's goals from lowest to highest and pick the middle one. This shows what a typical player scored. If one player scores a huge number of goals (or one scores zero), the median barely moves — it only cares about the middle player, not the extreme ones.

Mode — the goal count that shows up most often. Useful for spotting the "normal" performance across the squad, or if lots of players are stuck on the same low number.

Skew: when one or two players score way more (or way less) than everyone else, the mean gets pulled toward them, but the median stays close to the "normal" players. That gap between mean and median is exactly how you spot a skewed team — one where the numbers are lopsided because of a few standout (or struggling) performances.
