Secret Number and Pattern Train

Puzzles for your brain. Take your time. There's no clock.

Can you find my secret number?

    How to play. Read a clue. Tap every number that doesn't match it, to cross it out. You can slide your finger to cross out lots. Keep going until only one number is left. That's the secret number!

    One carriage is missing. Which one goes in the gap?

    Pattern tip. Look at one carriage, then the next. What changed? Does the pattern repeat, or does it grow by the same amount each time?
