How the four views fit together
  • School sets the order: the weekly pack follows whatever topic is happening in class.
  • The syllabus sets the depth: in Year 8 that means the skills GCSE will need (explaining, linking, judging), not GCSE content yet.
  • The grown-up sets the routine: time each week and the skills that keep coming back.
  • You set the priorities: your ambers and reds decide what gets extra attention.
Explore journeys (like trigonometry from scratch) are never part of this deal.
