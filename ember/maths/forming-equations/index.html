Forming and solving equations

Turn a story into algebra, then solve it. Start at 1 and work along.

The 5-step method for every problem
  1. Read it, and underline what you are asked to find at the end.
  2. Let x = the thing everything else is compared to.
  3. Write every other quantity using x (words to algebra).
  4. Find what is equal (a total, a perimeter, angles adding to 180°) and write the equation.
  5. Solve, then answer the question asked, then check it back in the story.
